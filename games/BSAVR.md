In a world where strategy meets survival, the Brigade Support Area (BSA) VR simulation puts you behind the front lines. Soldiers coordinate the food, water, and munitions that keep a brigade fighting, and build defenses like dug-outs and concertina wire, all inside an immersive training environment that pushes the limits of hands-on learning.

Trainees traverse a 12km &times; 12km terrain across a variety of training scenarios, a serious performance envelope for standalone VR. Working with our technical artist, we leveraged LODs and aggressive optimization to hold acceptable performance on Quest 3 hardware across the full play space.

Training doesn't end when the headset comes off. User results stream to the instructor's PC in real time, letting instructors coach mid-exercise and run after action reports (AARs) to turn each session into concrete learning experiences.

## Design challenges

Building BSA VR meant solving some of VR's most interesting problems: UI and inventory navigation that works with motion controllers under pressure, deep performance optimization for mobile-class hardware, and a seamless first-time user experience for trainees who may have never worn a headset before.
